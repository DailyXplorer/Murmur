# Murmur 1.7.7

Meetings
- Speakers are told apart reliably again: a short system sound no longer turns every voice of an in-person meeting into "Me", and on calls your own voice stays "Me" even when the other side speaks little.
- "Transcribe Again" no longer replaces a complete transcript, its summary and your speaker names when Codex can't answer.
- Meeting titles never show raw speaker markers, including titles saved before.
- A Discard dialog left open can no longer delete the next meeting's recording, and the Discard button no longer covers the recording title.
- Meetings follow your Simplified or Traditional Chinese choice.
- Copy Summary and Copy Transcript work on older macOS versions.

Dictation and settings
- With Always-On Microphone or Keep Mic Open on the default microphone, dictation keeps recording when that device goes away.
- A damaged settings file no longer resets your settings or clears your history.
- The silent-microphone alert only shows when the microphone really records silence, with the right fix when permission is denied.
- An expired Antigravity session is named when a Gemini transcription fails.
- The "Learned" badge in Custom Words is visible again.
