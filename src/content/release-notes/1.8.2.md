# Murmur 1.8.2

New

- Dictation styles: pick Formal, Normal or Casual in Settings > Transcription or during setup. A style changes punctuation and wording, never the meaning.
- Sign in with ChatGPT right from Murmur, in your browser. You no longer need to install the Codex app. Settings > Transcription shows the connection and lets you sign out.
- Better context: cleanup also reads a short excerpt of the text around your cursor, so names are spelled right and a sentence you are continuing keeps its capitalisation. Password fields and password managers are never read, and nothing is stored.
- On Macs with Apple silicon, the local model now listens for your custom words, so they come out right even before cleanup.

Design

- "AI cleanup and formatting" is now "Cleanup and formatting".
