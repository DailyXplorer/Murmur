# Murmur 1.8.1

Fixes

- The Dock icon now keeps your accent colour after launch and when the settings window reopens.
- Dictating on the Finder desktop no longer creates a text file there.
- When there is no text field to paste into, a small panel says so and offers a Copy button. Your clipboard is only changed when you click it.

Design

- "Update available" now shows as a pill in your accent colour.
- The local model row in Settings > Transcription is shorter.
- The ChatGPT step of setup is simpler: Start, Retry and Skip.
- Settings > About and Custom words show less text.

Languages

- The interface is now available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese. If you used another language, Murmur switches to English. Dictation still works in every language it supported before.
