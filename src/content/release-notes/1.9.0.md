# Murmur 1.9.0

Meetings

- Murmur now offers to record when a call starts in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, FaceTime, Webex or Discord, in the app or in a browser tab. Nothing is recorded until you click Record. You can turn this off in Settings > Advanced.
- Meetings are much harder to lose. Audio is written to disk as you record, a meeting cut off by a crash or a forced quit is transcribed at the next launch, and a lost connection no longer fails a meeting: Murmur waits and continues once you are back online, or finishes on this Mac with the local model.
- A meeting stops at 3 hours as one recording with one summary, with a warning a few minutes before.
- Your Mac no longer goes to sleep during a meeting while it is idle, and you are told if it slept anyway.
- Fewer duplicated sentences under "Me" when a call plays on your speakers, even without the on-device models.
- New in Settings > Advanced: optionally delete meeting audio after 7, 30 or 90 days, and see how much space it uses. Transcripts and summaries are kept.

Updates

- Murmur now checks for updates every 4 hours and when your Mac wakes, and shows "Update available" in the menu bar menu too. Nothing installs without you.
- A new installer window shows where to drag Murmur.

Dictation

- The text around your cursor is sent to cleanup as before, without the old filtering of codes and passwords. Password fields and password managers are still never read.

Fixes

- Clearer error lines in History and in meeting summaries, with a Retry button.
- Many fixes to local transcription, custom-word boosting, onboarding, model downloads and settings.
