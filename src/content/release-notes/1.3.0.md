# Murmur 1.3.0

Murmur now cleans up your dictation before pasting it.

- Fillers and hesitations are removed, and self-corrections are applied:
  "2 pm… actually 3 pm" becomes "3 pm".
- Punctuation and capitalisation are fixed, and spoken lists become lists.
  Your words and your language are kept; Murmur never answers or translates.
- If the cleanup fails or takes too long, the raw transcription is pasted, so
  you never lose a dictation. History keeps both versions.
- Murmur learns from your corrections: when you fix the spelling of a name
  right after a dictation, it is added to your dictionary. Learned words are
  listed in Settings, where you can remove them or add your own.
- Proper names visible in the active window help Murmur spell them right.
  Only the names are sent, password fields and password managers are never
  read, and nothing is kept.
- Whisper mode: quiet speech is boosted automatically, and a setting raises
  the sensitivity further for very low voices.

The cleanup uses the same Codex session as transcription, and you can turn it
off in Settings.
