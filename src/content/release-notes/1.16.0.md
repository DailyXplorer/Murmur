# Murmur 1.16.0

Dictation

- A kind of app can now have its own dictation language. In Settings, under Transcription, "Language by app type" has one menu each for AI prompts, personal messages, work messages, emails, documents, and coding and terminal apps: French in your messages and English in your code editor, for example. A kind of app left on "Same as general" keeps the language chosen in General.
- "Mute while recording" now leaves headphones alone. The microphone cannot hear what they play, so your music keeps going while you dictate. Speakers are muted as before. An Intel Mac cannot tell headphones from speakers and still mutes both.

First run

- The last step of the setup now shows your first dictation: hold your shortcut, speak, and your words appear in the window.

Command line

- `murmur --transcribe-file` now reads MP3, M4A, AIFF, CAF and FLAC files as well as WAV, up to 15 minutes.

Windows (beta)

Murmur for Windows is a beta: download `Murmur_1.16.0_x64-setup.exe` from the same page as the Mac app. The installer has no Windows certificate, so SmartScreen asks once. PCs on Beta 7 or 8 update by themselves.
