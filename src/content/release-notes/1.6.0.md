# Murmur 1.6.0

- A new, minimal recording bar: a dark capsule with a livelier waveform that
  grows with your voice. No buttons, badges or labels while you dictate, and
  hands-free looks the same as push-to-talk.
- While Murmur transcribes, the waveform settles into a soft shimmer.
- Error messages stay fully readable in every language.
- Press your cancel shortcut to cancel a dictation.
