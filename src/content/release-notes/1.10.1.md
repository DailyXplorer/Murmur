# Murmur 1.10.1

Meetings

- Murmur no longer quits when the call prompt closes. In 1.10.0 it quit when you clicked Not now, Record or Stop and transcribe, and when the prompt closed after the call ended.
- Murmur asks for every call again, with just Not now and Record. The Always and Never choices from 1.10.0 are gone, so a call app you set to Always record now waits for your click.

Fixes

- Settings rows show their explanation in the (i) bubble next to the title, instead of text under it.
- Library updates.
