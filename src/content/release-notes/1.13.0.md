# Murmur 1.13.0

Murmur for Windows now ships in the same release as the Mac app, under the same version number.

Dictation

- A cancelled dictation can be brought back with Restore for a minute, from a pill or from the menu bar.
- Short dictations are pasted at once, and fillers and spoken formatting are fixed without AI.
- Murmur now transcribes on your Mac with Parakeet Ultra. Custom words are no longer learned from your corrections.

Meetings

- Read a meeting live while it records, and find any meeting or dictation by its words with search.
- Murmur tells you when a meeting's transcript is ready, and offers to stop and transcribe when the recorded call ends.
- Murmur now offers to record calls held in Helium, Vivaldi, Opera, Chromium, Dia and Comet.
- Delete moves a meeting to the Trash instead of erasing it for good.
- A new read-only `murmur mcp` server lets AI assistants read your meetings. It is off until you turn it on in Settings.

Windows (beta)

Murmur for Windows is a beta and comes with this release: download `Murmur_1.13.0_x64-setup.exe` from the same page as the Mac app. The installer has no Windows certificate, so SmartScreen asks once. PCs on Beta 7 or 8 update by themselves. Nothing changes for Mac users.
