# Murmur 1.1.0

Murmur now has an Insights page in the main window.

- See your speaking speed in words per minute over the last 30 days.
- Track the total number of words you have dictated.
- Count the fixes Murmur made for you, such as custom-word corrections.
- See which kinds of apps you dictate into: AI prompts, messages, emails,
  documents, coding and more.
- Keep your streak going: current and longest streaks, plus a calendar of the
  days you dictated.

Everything stays on your Mac. App categories are recorded from this version
onwards; earlier dictations still count toward your words and streaks.
