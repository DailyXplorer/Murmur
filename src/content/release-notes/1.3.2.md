# Murmur 1.3.2

This update fixes more issues found in a second full review.

- Typing in the History limit field no longer deletes history before you
  finish the number.
- Pasting works in apps that macOS reports without a bundle identifier.
- An invalid Cancel shortcut is refused, so Escape keeps cancelling, and
  shortcuts with Command can be recorded in one go.
- Long Gemini recordings get a transcription time limit that grows with their
  length.
- Your clipboard is restored even if you quit Murmur right after a paste.
- AI cleanup no longer rejects correct text because of a short custom word, an
  elided name ("l'iPhone", "d'OpenAI") or a window name.
- Learned words no longer rewrite a brand inside a web address or delete a
  real word, and custom words keep their plural or possessive ending.
- Window names stop being sent as soon as you turn the feature off.
- Rewriting a whole chat message no longer counts as corrected words in
  Insights.
