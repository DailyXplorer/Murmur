# Murmur 1.7.8

What's new

- Insights now shows the time dictation saved you, in place of the "Fixes made" card.
- Codex is now the only transcription and cleanup service: Gemini (Antigravity) and Apple Intelligence are gone, and settings that used them switch to Codex automatically.

Meetings

- Your own words stay under "Me": a reply, a quick confirmation or a repeat of what the other side said is no longer removed with their voice picked up by your microphone.
- A denied System Audio Recording permission is flagged when a meeting starts, before a whole call is recorded without the other side.
- A deleted meeting's transcript can no longer end up in the next meeting.
- Summaries connect on slow networks, and Retry on a failed meeting shows its progress while another meeting is processing.
- Double-clicking Start, Stop, Separate speakers, Retry or the speaker model buttons no longer runs the action twice.
- Sentences no longer split at titles like "M." or at spaced « » quotes, and Chinese and Japanese sentences no longer get a space between them.

Dictation

- Quitting Murmur or logging out during a dictation no longer leaves your Mac muted, and quitting no longer signs you out of Codex.
- Dictating into Spotlight, Raycast or Alfred pastes into the panel, not the app behind it.
- Changing the Cancel shortcut while dictating no longer freezes Murmur, and shortcut keys show what your keyboard layout prints, so the meeting shortcut works on French keyboards.

Custom words

- Custom words with accents are applied, and short names and first-name spellings are corrected.
- Custom words no longer rewrite everyday words ("sinon" into "Simon") or drop a French elision ("l'iPhone").
- Learned names no longer keep a possessive or an elision ("D'Anthropic"), and whole Chinese or Japanese sentences are no longer learned as words.
- Double-clicking a custom word removes only that word.

Settings

- Languages and the default microphone are named in the app's language.
- Auto-Delete Recordings says it removes the transcriptions too, and switching microphone during a hands-free dictation says why it was refused.
- Double-clicking the History trash deletes only one dictation, and a dialog opened by a double click stays open.
- Granting a permission again takes you back to the app instead of the Codex sign-in.
