# Murmur 1.2.0

One shortcut now does both kinds of dictation.

- Hold the shortcut to talk, and release it to stop.
- Double-tap the shortcut to dictate hands-free. Press it once more to stop.
  The recording overlay shows when hands-free is on.
- Paste your last transcription again with a new shortcut (⌃⌘V by default) or
  from the menu bar, if it landed in the wrong place.
- Retry a failed dictation from History without saying it again: Murmur
  re-sends the saved recording. Successful entries can be transcribed again.

The separate push-to-talk setting is gone: your shortcut keeps working, and a
double tap replaces the old toggle mode.
