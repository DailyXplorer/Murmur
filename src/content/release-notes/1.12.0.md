# Murmur 1.12.0

Dictation

- A dictation pasted right after a word now gets a space before it, so "already writtenNext sentence" is gone. It works in apps that expose their cursor to macOS. To turn it off, use "Add leading space" in Transcription settings.

Meetings

- A pill now tells you when a meeting's transcript is ready. Open goes to the meeting and Copy copies the transcript. If a transcript fails, the pill says so.

Fixes

- A dictation or a meeting analysis no longer fails when it starts right as the switch to Parakeet Ultra finishes.
- Escape now closes a pill while Murmur is the active app.
