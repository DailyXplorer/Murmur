# Murmur 1.7.5

- Settings look more consistent: switches and the colour picker use the same
  light knob as the sliders, and the selected accent colour has a small,
  quiet ring.
- Antigravity and Custom Words put their status, field and button on a
  second line, so nothing is squeezed.
- The History entries' actions sit together, like in a meeting note.
- Insights is simpler: no subtitles under the sections, and "Words per
  minute" shows in full.
