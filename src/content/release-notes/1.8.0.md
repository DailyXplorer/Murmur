# Murmur 1.8.0

What's new

- Murmur can now turn your voice into text right on your Mac. It's free, has no quota, works offline, and your audio never leaves your Mac.
- It understands 25 European languages, including English and French, and adds punctuation on its own.
- It needs a one-time download of about 500 MB (about 740 MB on Intel Macs). New installs offer it first. If you already use Murmur, a card in Settings lets you switch whenever you like.
- The cloud is still one click away in Settings > Transcription, with more languages. A ChatGPT account is now optional: connect one for AI cleanup and for languages the Mac doesn't cover.
- If one way can't transcribe a dictation, Murmur tries the other when it can, and History tells you which one did.

Dictation

- When no text field is selected, Murmur copies your dictation and shows a Copy button, so nothing is lost.

Settings

- The settings window opens larger, so more fits without scrolling.
- Labels and buttons use sentence case everywhere, with no all-capital text.
