# Murmur 1.14.0

Dictation

- Dictation starts and finishes faster, and Murmur keeps listening for a moment after you let go so the last word is not cut.
- Say "open quote", "close quote" and spoken symbols such as "at sign" or "hashtag", in English and French, and Murmur writes them.
- If a call app takes your microphone, Murmur reopens it, so the next dictation is not lost.
- On Mac, dictation now pastes into remote-desktop and virtual-machine windows.

History

- Transcribe again can run in a language you pick for that one time, and earlier versions of a dictation are kept so you can copy or restore them.
- Each dictation shows whether its text was made on your Mac or PC, or with ChatGPT.
- Clear all history now keeps your starred dictations, and delete buttons that cannot be undone are red.

Meetings

- Import an audio file (WAV, MP3, M4A, AIFF, CAF or FLAC) as a meeting, and get a transcript, title, summary and search for it like any other meeting.
- Meetings record the microphone the call app is using.
- Meeting titles and summaries now use your custom words.

Insights

- A new Share button turns your stats into one square picture you can save or copy.

Windows (beta)

Murmur for Windows is a beta and comes with this release: download `Murmur_1.14.0_x64-setup.exe` from the same page as the Mac app. The installer has no Windows certificate, so SmartScreen asks once. PCs on Beta 7 or 8 update by themselves. Large files such as models and recordings now live in your local app data folder, so they no longer sync with your roaming profile. Nothing else changes for Mac users.
