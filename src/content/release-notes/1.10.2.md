# Murmur 1.10.2

Overlay

- Dictation now shows a small orb. Its ring moves with your voice, and a light runs round it while Murmur transcribes.
- Every message opens from the orb, with a small sign of what it is about, and shows one at a time. If your microphone disconnects and there is no text field to paste into, the Copy pill comes first and the microphone warning follows it.
- Call prompts are shorter: "Record this meeting?" and "Call ended.", on one line.
- In the last 5 minutes before the 3-hour limit, the meeting time turns red.

Fixes

- In Settings > Advanced > AI assistants, the Copy button lines up with the other actions.
