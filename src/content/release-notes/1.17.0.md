# Murmur 1.17.0

Meetings

- A speaker whose name is said in the meeting is now shown under it: "this is Marie", or someone calls them by name and they answer. The others stay "Speaker 2" until you rename them, and a name you gave is never replaced.
- A meeting can now be transcribed on your computer. In Settings, under Advanced, "On-device meeting transcript" lets the local model write the transcript, so the meeting's audio stays on your computer and only the text goes to ChatGPT, for the title and the summary. It is off by default. ChatGPT still transcribes when the local model is not ready, or when your language is not one of the 25 it covers.

Settings

- The style and the language of each kind of app now share one row. In Transcription, under "By app type", Customize… opens the six kinds of app with their style and their language side by side.

Cloud transcription

- The audio Murmur sends to ChatGPT is now about half the size, with no loss of quality. Dictations and meetings use less data, and upload faster on a slow connection.
- Murmur makes fewer requests. It checks for updates at launch and every four hours, no longer each time the Mac wakes, and it no longer prepares a connection to ChatGPT that it already has.

Privacy

- The log no longer holds the names of your microphones and speakers.

Updates

- On a PC, this window now shows what an update brought, as on the Mac. "Show what's new" in Settings, under Advanced, turns it off.

Windows (beta)

Murmur for Windows is a beta: download `Murmur_1.17.0_x64-setup.exe` from the same page as the Mac app. The installer has no Windows certificate, so SmartScreen asks once. PCs on Beta 7 or 8 update by themselves.
