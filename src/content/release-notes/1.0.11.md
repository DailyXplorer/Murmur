# Murmur 1.0.11

This update makes service setup, cancellation, and text insertion more reliable.

- Choose Codex or Antigravity during setup, with clearer configuration status,
  translated errors, and actions to help you connect a service. Antigravity
  remains experimental and detects the spoken language automatically.
- Cancel transcription work while it is processing, and prevent cancelled or
  outdated results from appearing later. Pressing the toggle shortcut again
  also cancels a recording start that is still waiting for the microphone.
- Keep text insertion responsive, check the destination before pasting, and
  report clipboard failures while retaining recoverable transcripts in history.
- Shut down more cleanly during transcription and avoid duplicating the audio
  buffer while saving a recording.
- Limit recordings to 15 minutes and improve validation of settings and audio
  files.
