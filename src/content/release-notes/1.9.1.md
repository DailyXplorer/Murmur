# Murmur 1.9.1

Dictation

- If your microphone disconnects during a dictation (USB unplugged, Bluetooth headset turned off), Murmur now says so: "Recording stopped early: your microphone disconnected." What was recorded before the cut is still pasted, or offered with Copy.

Meetings

- Murmur now offers to record calls held in Helium, Vivaldi, Opera, Chromium, Dia and Comet, as it already did in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Brave and Firefox.

Fixes

- Radio buttons in Settings now match the toggles, and the sidebar spacing under the logo matches the gap between tabs.
- Library updates.
