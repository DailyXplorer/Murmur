# Murmur 1.10.0

Dictation

- Short dictations are pasted at once, with fillers removed and spoken formatting applied, without waiting for AI cleanup.
- Local dictation starts faster and stays fast: the model is warmed up ahead of time and rebuilt after a macOS update.
- Long dictations on this Mac no longer lose sentences, and French text no longer drifts into English words. Murmur sends your dictation language to the on-device model and listens again to any stretch of speech it skipped.
- Pressed Escape by mistake? A cancelled dictation is kept in memory for 60 seconds. Click Restore in the overlay or in the menu bar menu to bring it back. The audio is never written to disk.
- Murmur no longer learns new dictionary words from your corrections. Words it added that way are removed. Words you add yourself are kept.

Meetings

- Read a meeting while it records. A live view shows what was said a few seconds ago, made on this Mac. A new "Paste new meeting text" shortcut, unbound until you pick keys, pastes the lines said since you last used it.
- Choose per call app whether Murmur asks, always records or never asks, from the start prompt or in Settings > Advanced.
- When the call you recorded ends, Murmur offers to stop the meeting and transcribe it.
- Search every meeting and dictation by its words.
- Let an AI assistant such as Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop list, search and read your meetings. Off by default, read-only, and set up in Settings > Advanced > AI assistants. Dictations stay hidden unless you turn on a second switch.

Fixes

- Murmur keeps fewer hidden windows in memory, so the menu bar uses less.
- Settings are saved safely even if Murmur is interrupted, and a crash now leaves a log to help diagnose it.
- Spoken commands such as "new line" or "question mark" stay words when they are part of a phrase like "a new line of products".
- Offline while the local model is being optimized for a new macOS version? Dictation now waits for it instead of failing.
- Library updates and security fixes.
