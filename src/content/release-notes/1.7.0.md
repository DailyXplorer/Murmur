# Murmur 1.7.0

- New: meeting notes. Start a meeting from the menu bar or with
  Control+Option+Command+M. Murmur records your microphone and the sound of
  your Mac, and shows a red pill with the elapsed time. Meetings stop on
  their own after 3 hours.
- When the meeting ends, Murmur transcribes it with Codex, tells the
  speakers apart, and writes a title and a summary. Each note has a Summary
  tab and a Transcript tab that you can search, copy and export as Markdown.
- Click a speaker's name to rename them everywhere in the note. Your
  microphone is always "Me".
- The audio of each meeting is kept on your Mac in a compressed file, and
  you can delete it from the note.
- If Codex is busy, Murmur waits and tries again by itself, and a Retry
  button lets you start again sooner.
- Speaker detection needs macOS 15 on Apple silicon. Recording the sound of
  your Mac needs macOS 14.4; on older systems, meetings use the microphone
  only.
- Fixes: starred dictations are kept when old history is cleaned up, and
  history retention now also runs at launch and every hour. Signing out of
  Codex during a token refresh no longer signs you back in.
