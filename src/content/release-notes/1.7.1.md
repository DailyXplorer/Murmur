# Murmur 1.7.1

- A deleted meeting no longer comes back in the list for a moment.
- The 3-hour limit and the timer in the pill now count the same time, even
  after the Mac sleeps.
- Murmur's own sounds stay out of the recording of your Mac's audio.
- If the microphone gives no sound when a meeting starts, Murmur warns you.
- Speaker models you deleted or stopped downloading are no longer
  downloaded again at every meeting.
- Meeting audio uploads give up less often on a slow connection.
- Codex errors during a summary or a cleanup are reported more precisely,
  so a rate limit waits instead of failing.
- Small fixes to meeting search and to VoiceOver labels in Settings.
