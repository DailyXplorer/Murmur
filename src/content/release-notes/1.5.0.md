# Murmur 1.5.0

- AI cleanup can now run on Apple Intelligence, on your Mac: free, unlimited,
  offline and private. It is the new default. Codex stays available in
  Settings → AI cleanup.
- Settings show whether Apple Intelligence is ready on this Mac and, if not,
  why (turned off, model still downloading, language not supported).
- When Apple Intelligence cannot clean a dictation (language not supported,
  system limit reached), Murmur uses Codex if you are signed in, otherwise it
  pastes your dictation as spoken.
