# Murmur 1.0.9

This update makes recording, cancellation, settings, and history more reliable.

- Cancel remains available while transcription finishes, and cancelled results
  are not saved or pasted.
- Microphone, channel, and recording mode changes only take effect after audio
  reconfiguration succeeds.
- History stays consistent when entries arrive, change, or are deleted while
  pages load.
- Settings changes no longer overwrite newer choices during startup or rapid
  updates.
- Clipboard restoration preserves content copied by another app while Murmur
  is working.
- Accessibility labels, keyboard controls, update checks, and permission
  recovery are more dependable.
- Startup commands sent to an already launching Murmur instance are queued
  instead of being lost.
