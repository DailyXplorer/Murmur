# Murmur 1.7.3

- The first meeting after allowing System Audio Recording now records the
  other participants. Before, the recording that was running when you
  clicked Allow kept capturing silence.
- Meeting summaries work on every ChatGPT plan. Murmur now asks Codex which
  models your plan can use, and falls back to the fast model if one is
  refused.
- When a meeting has no sound from other apps, the message says why: it
  points to the privacy settings only when the permission is really
  missing.
- Audio files under 1 MB show their size in kB instead of "0 MB".
