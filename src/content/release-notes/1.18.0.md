# Murmur 1.18.0

Dictation

- With ChatGPT transcription, your text now comes back about half a second after you stop, however long you spoke. Murmur sends the audio while you speak instead of once you have finished. In our measurements, a dictation of almost four minutes took 9 seconds to come back and now takes under one. Short dictations were already that fast. When the connection is slow or ChatGPT turns the stream down, the recording is sent as before.
- You can now read your words while you dictate. In Settings, under Advanced, "Show text while dictating" shows them next to the recording orb as ChatGPT hears them. It is off by default and works with ChatGPT transcription only. The text that is pasted does not change.
- On a Mac, "Mute while recording" no longer adds about a tenth of a second to each dictation.

Privacy

- Because the audio now leaves while you speak, a dictation you cancel has already been sent to ChatGPT up to that point, and dictating uses about three times the data it did. To send the recording only once you stop, as before, turn off "Stream dictations to ChatGPT" in the Debug settings (Cmd+Shift+D, or Ctrl+Shift+D on a PC).
- Signing in to ChatGPT from Murmur no longer creates an API key. Murmur never used it, and a key an earlier sign-in saved is removed the next time you sign in.

Settings

- In "By app type", the note about styles now sits in the (i) next to the window's title instead of under the menus.
- About and the sidebar now have a "Give a star" button. It opens Murmur's page on GitHub, where a star helps other people find the project.

First run

- When macOS asks for the microphone, the prompt now says what Murmur uses it for: your dictations and the meetings you record.

Windows (beta)

Murmur for Windows is a beta: download `Murmur_1.18.0_x64-setup.exe` from the same page as the Mac app. The installer has no Windows certificate, so SmartScreen asks once. PCs on Beta 7 or 8 update by themselves.
